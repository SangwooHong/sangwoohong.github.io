Research

Reliable AI from foundations to deployment.

TML studies reliability from computational foundations, through foundation models, to AI systems operating in the physical world. Reliable and safe algorithms connect all three levels.

Research Areas

Reliable Computing

Computational foundations for reliable AI, spanning efficient execution, distributed systems, verifiability, and next-generation computing.

  • Efficient Computing
  • Distributed Computing
  • Verifiable Computing
  • Quantum / Next-Gen Computing
