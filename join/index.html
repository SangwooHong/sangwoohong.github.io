Join TML

Students interested in trustworthy AI are welcome to contact us.

TML looks for students who enjoy rigorous research, careful engineering, and problems that connect AI reliability with real systems.

신뢰할 수 있는 AI, Foundation Models, 자율주행/VLA, 언러닝, 프라이버시, 양자/분산 계산 기반 연구에 관심 있는 학생들의 연락을 환영합니다.

Open Positions

TML welcomes inquiries from prospective graduate students and undergraduate researchers interested in trustworthy AI. Graduate study may include bachelor's-master's integrated, master's, and master's-Ph.D. integrated tracks.

Research opportunities connect to reliable computing, foundation models, autonomous driving, machine unlearning, quantum machine learning, and safe generative AI.

대학원 진학, 학석사연계, 석사, 석박통합, 학부 연구생에 관심 있는 학생은 관심 연구 주제와 수행한 프로젝트, 논문, 코드 경험을 간단히 정리해 연락해 주세요.

What We Look For

  • Strong interest in trustworthy machine learning
  • Careful reading and writing habits
  • Solid programming and experimental discipline
  • Willingness to work across theory, systems, and applications

Research Areas

  • Reliable computing and verifiable computation
  • LLMs, generative AI, and multimodal models
  • Autonomous driving, Vision-Language-Action (VLA), and embodied AI
  • Unlearning, privacy, robustness, alignment, and explainability

Location

  • Professor office: Engineering Building A, Room 1513
  • Student lab: New Engineering Building, Room 1206

Send a concise email describing your background, research interests, and relevant projects or papers.
