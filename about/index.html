Biography

Sangwoo Hong has been an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering at Konkuk University since 2025.

He received his bachelor's degree in 2020 and his Ph.D. in 2024, both from the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University.

Research Interests

  • Privacy: data privacy and privacy-preserving generative models
  • Security: verifiable computing and adversarial robustness
  • Fairness: max-min fairness and counterfactual fairness
  • Reliability: distributed computing and network pruning
  • Explainability: explainable AI

Service

  • ACL 2026, Session Chair
  • NeurIPS, Area Chair
  • AAAI, Program Committee
  • Reviewer for NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, and ISIT
  • Reviewer for TNNLS, TIFS, and TCOM
